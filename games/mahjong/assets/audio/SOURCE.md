# Mahjong discard sound

`discard-sound.js` contains an edited excerpt of **牌を置く・その３**
by T-STUDIO / みんなの創作支援サイトＴスタ.

- Source catalogue: <https://tnosite.com/embed/>
- Free asset pack: <https://t-studio-tst.itch.io/free-sound-mahjong-sound-pack>
- Terms: <https://tnosite.com/agreement/>

The source permits free commercial and non-commercial use, with attribution
optional. It prohibits selling the material by itself, registering it with
Content ID or rights-management services, and claiming authorship.

Edits made for Playweft Games: retained one contact from the original
two-contact placement, converted to mono, lowered its pitch slightly, applied
a low-pass filter and short decay to match a felt-covered table, and trimmed
the result to a 172 ms WAV one-shot.
