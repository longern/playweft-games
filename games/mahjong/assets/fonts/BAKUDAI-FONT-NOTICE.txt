Bakudai Brush Font / 莫大毛筆字體

Source: https://github.com/max32002/bakudaifont
Upstream version used for this subset: 1.48 Regular

Bakudai is derived from Aoyagi Kouzan's Kouzan Brush Font Gyousho. This
project embeds a WOFF2 subset containing only the characters required by
the Mahjong match label and action buttons.
